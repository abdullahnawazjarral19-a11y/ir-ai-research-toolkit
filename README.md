
IR-AI Research Toolkit

An open-source toolkit for International Relations research, AI-assisted literature reviews, geopolitical analysis, and academic research workflows.

About

This project is being developed to support students and researchers in International Relations and Political Science.

It aims to provide practical resources for organizing academic research, conducting literature reviews, and exploring responsible applications of artificial intelligence in research.

Objectives

- Organize academic literature reviews.
- Support research methodology and academic writing.
- Explore AI-assisted research workflows.
- Develop tools for geopolitical and foreign policy analysis.
- Encourage collaboration and knowledge sharing.

Planned Features

Literature Review Organizer

A structured way to organize research papers, authors, arguments, and research gaps.

Research Methodology Resources

Templates for qualitative research, case studies, comparative analysis, and content analysis.

Geopolitical Analysis

Frameworks for studying international conflicts, security issues, and foreign policy.

AI Research Workflows

Examples of responsible AI use in academic research.

Development Status

This project is in its initial development stage.

Features listed above are planned and will be developed progressively.

Contributing

Suggestions, feedback, and contributions are welcome.

Please open an issue to discuss ideas before submitting a pull request.

Responsible Research

AI-generated information should be verified using reliable academic sources. Users should independently verify facts, references, and research findings.
